Step 3 · What's Hot

What our hand-picked Twitter accounts are saying

"Watch this week. Not ready for money yet."

What this is, in one paragraph: we built a robot that reads Twitter/X every single day for us. It watches about 150 hand-picked crypto accounts — traders and analysts we actually respect — and counts every coin they mention. Then it hands us a summary of what's bubbling up.

Why counting works: one person hyping a coin means nothing. Twenty of them, in the same week, means something is happening. So we sort everything into three buckets by how long the buzz has lasted:

🌱 Brand newNobody has said a word about it for a month — and today somebody did.
📈 About a week of buzzA real cluster of accounts has picked it up over the past 7 days.
🔥 Talked about constantlyIt keeps coming up, week after week, from lots of different accounts.

Brand new = highest risk, earliest look. Constant talk = safest attention, but you're late to it. The sweet spot is usually the middle bucket.
