Step 2 · The money

If this system had $1,000,000 today

The dollars are only there to make the percentages easy to picture. What matters is the split — and that cash counts as a real position, not a leftover.

All three risk levels look at the same facts — they just care about different ones. Low risk asks "is Bitcoin cheap?" before anything else, so it moves slowly and keeps the most cash. High risk is the momentum setting: it barely cares about cheap — if the trend is up it stays deployed, and it only steps back when the trend actually breaks. Picking it means you have already accepted the big dips. Cash is still a real position, and the alt-coin door only opens as money actually rotates in — high risk just opens it first. Medium is the default — that is where the family's caution lives.

What this is, in one paragraph: the Twitter Scraper reads X/Twitter every day for us. It follows — hand-picked crypto accounts — traders and analysts worth listening to — and counts every coin they mention. That count is the whole trick: one person hyping a coin means nothing. Twenty of them in the same week means something is happening.

🌱 Brand newNobody said a word about it for a month — and today somebody did.
📈 About a week of buzzA real cluster of accounts picked it up over the past 7 days.
🔥 Talked about constantlyIt keeps coming up, week after week, from lots of different accounts.

Brand new = highest risk, earliest look. Constant talk = safest attention, but you are late. The middle bucket is usually the sweet spot.
