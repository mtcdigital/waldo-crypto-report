🔬 Anatomy of the Four Scores

Exactly what feeds each 0–100 number on the AI Report tab — every input, its weight, and how the math works. Nothing hidden.

The one formula behind all four

Every input votes between −1 (very bearish) and +1 (very bullish) →
each vote is multiplied by its weight (how much we trust that input) →
the weighted average is mapped onto 0–100, where 50 = perfectly neutral.

So: 100 and 0 are hard walls — a score can never go past them. 39 means "the blend of votes leans mildly bearish." Weight = the evidence's own importance × the source's trust score from Step 4 (CCI 10/10, Cowen 10/10, market data 10/10, Giovanni 7/10, Twitter Scraper 5/10). Fresh quotes pulled daily from CCI's Discord and Cowen's videos join the vote and fade to zero over 21 days.
